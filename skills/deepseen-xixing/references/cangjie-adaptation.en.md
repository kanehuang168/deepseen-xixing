- [kangarooking/cangjie-skill](https://github.com/kangarooking/cangjie-skill/tree/a28de55ba881b9928956a55048f743f7a9e3b23e), declared 2.5.0; LICENSE at that revision is MIT. Reviewed entry, independent-entry decisions and delivery guidance.

Existing DeepSeen methods already covered source understanding, differences and boundary tests. This adaptation adds explicit destinations for important source material and separates retaining a method from creating another independently installed Skill. Stable method IDs support multiple presentations without divergent copies.

No third-party scripts, templates, long prompts or examples were copied or executed. Fixed five-agent workflows, repeated confirmations, fixed entry limits, impersonation and automatic evolution/release were not adopted.

The self-authored fixture retains two candidates, one reference and one rejected claim. Structural checks and tests cover missing coverage, duplicates, source changes and escaped/missing destinations. These do not establish method quality uplift, independent transfer performance or production activation. Future improvements need controlled comparisons and previous-success regression.
