# Fictional example: adapting a meeting-note method

All material and observations below are invented for illustration; this is not a customer result.

Goal: make next actions visible. Current practice: share a transcript. Authorized sample source: “Separate decisions from suggestions; name an owner for each action and mark an unknown deadline as pending.” The entire sample was read. The source proposes a method; improved completion is an untested hypothesis.

Keep the transcript and add a short decisions/actions/pending-discussion table. For “Try A; Lee collects materials; no deadline; discuss B later”, produce: decision: try A; action: Lee collects materials, deadline pending; discussion: B. A small trial would check accuracy, preparation time and whether owners recognize tasks. Keep the transcript and withdraw the table if inaccurate.

This is a paper walkthrough, not an executed comparison or proof of improved outcomes. Do not send or publish without authorization.
